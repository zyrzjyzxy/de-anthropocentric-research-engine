# DARE Paper: Positioning and Venues (as of 2026-10-17)

## 1. Decision

Publish DARE as a paper, workshop first. Main line: the DARE artifact plus its evaluation (see `2026-10-17-dare-benchmark-plan.md`). The autonomous v4 iteration log goes in as one section plus an appendix. Details are settled at writing time.

## 2. Self-Improvement Framing

The Claude Code and Codex workers that redesigned v4 ran with DARE loaded, and the human only relayed messages without making decisions. So DARE redesigned itself, and the self-improvement framing holds.

The paper must back this with log evidence: which DARE tactics were invoked during the redesign. Reviewers will check.

## 3. Main-Track Contribution

A product that works is unlikely to clear an ICLR / ICML main track on its own. It needs a finding that generalizes beyond DARE:

- **Comparison**: MechHypoBench (arXiv 2610.05197) found loose skill collections give no stable gain. If DARE wins at matched tokens, the finding is "structured methodology with contracts and gates works; loose skills do not".
- **Ablation**: remove gates or contracts and measure the drop. This turns a product demo into a scientific question.

Workshop first, then extend to a main-track paper once results are in. Many workshops are non-archival.
